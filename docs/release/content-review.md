## How to read the table

- **Current value** is what the shipped content does today. **Alternative** is the most plausible other choice, not a recommendation.
- **Evidence tags** as in PRD EXL-05: **H** = hypertrophy data, **EMG** = activation data only, **C** = convention. Credits: 1.0 direct, 0.5 synergist, 0.25 "unvalidated convention".
- **Overload suitability** is the MAP-03 factor (d), from 0 (cannot be made heavier or lighter in small steps) to 3 (fully loadable).
- **Standard variant** (`ranking.standard`, M5z): the form of a movement that programs prescribe by default, as opposed to a specialised variant chosen for a purpose. It is only a tie-break: among exercises with equal MAP-03 scores for a plan slot or a muscle list, standard ones come first, then the ID (PRD v0.8 PLN-05, MAP-03). The content tests require at least one standard exercise in every substitution group and none among the close variants of a competition lift (`variantOf`).
- Rows in **bold** in the standard-variant part are the notable choices.
- Fill in the **Decision** column with "approve" or the change you want. A change to a credit, a rating or the standard flag changes plan picks; the plan-matrix snapshot then shows the effect for review (technical concept §8.7.6).

## After the decisions

An agent applies the changes in `packages/content/data/exercises/*.yaml`, runs `pnpm content:build` (content version and hash, `ids.lock.json`, the inventory entries), and updates the plan-matrix snapshot deliberately, with the changed picks listed for the product owner (as M5z did). Approving everything as it is needs no change.
