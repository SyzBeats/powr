The first version of POWR, for the beta: a training planner and tracker that works offline and keeps your data on this phone. There is no account and no tracking.

## New
- A plan from a short setup: your goal, experience, training days, time per workout and equipment. You see the plan before anything is saved, and you can change it later in the plan editor.
- Workout logging with one tap per set done as planned, quick changes to weight and reps, a rest timer with alerts, and a suggested weight and reps for the next set that you confirm.
- Past workouts: log a workout you did without the app, and edit or delete logged ones.
- Progress: estimated 1-rep max trends, personal records, weekly sets per muscle, the body map, streaks, a calendar and strength scores.
- A "Why?" explanation for each suggestion and check, with the studies behind it, and "How POWR decides" in Settings.
- An exercise library of 190 exercises with search and filters, and your own exercises.
- Equipment profiles for the places you train, from a preset or your own list.
- Bodyweight log with a trend.
- Backups as a ZIP file that you keep, restore from a backup, and safety copies that POWR takes before a restore or a data update.
- Calendar export of your planned workouts as an .ics file.
- Light and dark themes, larger controls, and RPE or reps in reserve for effort.

## Known limitations
- Rest alerts while the screen is locked depend on your phone and browser. Settings, then Device support, shows what works on this phone.
- English only.
- The legal texts are drafts until their review is finished.
